Animals

Animals visit when the conditions are right: time of day, weather, how much moss there is, water nearby. Tap a visitor for coins, and the first time you meet it, crystals. Some live in only one world.

  • Robin

    Comes by day once there's moss.

    Lives in: Clearing, Brook, Forgotten town

  • Lizard

    Loves sunny rocks.

    Lives in: Clearing, Castle ruin, Forgotten town

  • Snail

    Comes out when it rains.

    Lives in: Clearing, Castle ruin, Brook

  • Frog

    Needs water, at night or in the rain.

    Lives in: Clearing, Brook

  • Black cat

    Wanders well-kept scenes at night.

    Lives in: Clearing, Castle ruin, Forgotten town

  • Hedgehog

    Arrives with the gardener hedgehog.

    Lives in: Every world

  • Wild rabbit

    Clearing, by day, once moss covers some ground.

    Lives in: Clearing

  • Squirrel

    Clearing, by day, from level 4.

    Lives in: Clearing

  • Raven

    Ruin, by day, on top of walls and stones.

    Lives in: Castle ruin

  • Owl

    Ruin, at night, on the walls.

    Lives in: Castle ruin

  • Otter

    Brook, always by the water.

    Lives in: Brook

  • Kingfisher

    Brook, by day and without rain, at the water’s edge.

    Lives in: Brook

  • Pigeon

    Town, by day, in the streets.

    Lives in: Forgotten town

  • Fox

    Town, at night, once moss covers the streets.

    Lives in: Forgotten town

  • Moon rabbit

    Moon garden. They say it has always lived there.

    Lives in: Moon garden

  • Crystal slug

    Moon garden, among the stones.

    Lives in: Moon garden

  • Fire salamander

    Abyss, on the hot stone.

    Lives in: Ember abyss

  • Magma blob

    Abyss, hopping near the lava.

    Lives in: Ember abyss

  • Cloud sheep

    Sky islands, grazing among the cloudflowers.

    Lives in: Sky islands

  • Wind hare

    Sky islands, when a gust goes by.

    Lives in: Sky islands
