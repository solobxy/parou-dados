The mine

The mine opens at level 9. It is a second game next to your garden: you dig the earth pixel by pixel where you tap, find chests, fossils and rubies, and go deeper and deeper. It keeps digging even while you are in the garden.

How the mine works

  1. Dig chambers

    You see each chamber floor from above. Tap the stones to break them, layer by layer: underneath there is silver, rubies, chests, finds and, now and then, gems.

  2. One after another

    Every blow weakens the chamber. When it caves in, or when you dig everything up, you keep the haul and go straight down to the next. No lanterns, no waiting.

  3. The tunnel and the phases

    Every blow also pushes the tunnel on the map, and your helpers break stones on their own. When the tunnel reaches the end of the phase, you go down to the next world.

  4. Cards and currencies

    Every 5 chambers you pick 1 of 3 cards that improve chambers for good. In the mine you earn silver, for pickaxes and upgrades, and rubies, for the mine's powers.

The worlds of the mine

Finish a stage and you go down to the next one. After the Moon, the Void never ends.

  • 1. Surface

    Soil, roots, clay and stone. Where it all begins.

  • 2. Crystal caves

    In the dark. Caves with glowing crystals and silver veins.

  • 3. Underground river

    Water, lava and ancient ruins. Where water meets lava, obsidian is born.

  • 4. Earth’s mantle

    Hot green rock, rivers of magma and the first diamonds.

  • 5. Earth’s core

    The centre of the planet: iron, nickel and gold, glowing with heat.

  • 6. Underworld

    Beneath the core: bones of giants, stones holding souls and ores that do not exist on Earth.

  • 7. The Moon

    A portal in the heart of the underworld opens onto the Moon. Dust, craters and helium crystals.

  • 8. Moon’s core

    The cold heart of the Moon, full of moonstone.

  • 9. The Void

    Beyond the Moon there is nothing... except fallen stars. It never ends.
