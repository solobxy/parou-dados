Pets

Pets open at level 3 with a rare egg that hatches in 3 minutes, and every tap in the garden warms it. Your pet follows your gardener and gives a bonus in every scene while it is fed and happy. The rarer the pet, the bigger the bonus, and it grows as the pet levels up.

Eggs

Fusion

Five pets of the same rarity make one of the next rarity. Pets with rare colours raise the chance that the new pet hatches in colour. The accessories of the chosen pets go back to your inventory before they fuse.

Bloodline pets

At level 5 you choose one of three bloodlines: Sun, Moon or Root. Each one has a pet that only exists for the players who chose it.

Fossil dino

Complete the Lourinhã dinosaur in the mine and a fossil box opens with this pet inside, in a random colour. Every 20 more bones you find after that bring another box, with another chance at a rarer colour.
