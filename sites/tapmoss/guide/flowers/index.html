Flowers

Flowers bloom on dense moss. Rarer ones show up at night, and more of them bloom in spring and with upgrades like the botanist’s eye. Each world has six flowers, one for each rarity. Picking a new one raises your income in every scene, from +1% for a common flower to +20% for a mythic one.
