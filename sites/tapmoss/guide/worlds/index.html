Worlds

Every world is a new scene that earns on its own. Upgrades bought in one world only count there, and each world bends the rules a little.

Clearing

Free, your first scene

Rocks, trees and, if you're lucky, a pond.

Upgrades only count in this scene. The clearing is balanced: everything works as usual.

Unique upgrade: Beehive

6% more flowers bloom, and rarer ones

Castle ruin

Opens at level 20 · Costs 2,500,000 coins

Tall walls and a fallen tower. Moss loves stone.

Upgrades only count in this scene. In the ruin, the stone makes moss earn more, taps worth more and luck better, but moss grows slower and there are fewer flowers.

Castle restoration

  1. Clear the rubble: The walls stop looking so broken.
  2. Raise the walls: The walls get taller and straighter.
  3. Rebuild the tower: The tower gets its height and battlements back.
  4. Wooden gate: The arch gets a new gate.
  5. Banners: Banners on the walls.
  6. Castle complete: A flag on the tower and torches lit at night.
