A little forest where moss grows while you live your day

tapmoss is a free, cozy idle game in pixel art. You tap to spread spores, the moss slowly takes over stones, logs and old walls, and your garden keeps earning while you are away. The sky in the game is your sky: real day, night and weather, from your city.

Play for free

No download. Works on phone, tablet and computer.

How it plays

  1. Tap the ground

    Every tap gives coins and spreads spores. Moss never grows where you tap: it spreads on its own, slowly and at random, so every garden turns out different.

  2. Let it grow

    Moss covers stones, logs and walls and earns coins on its own, even with the game closed. Water it and it grows twice as fast for a while.

  3. Collect and care

    Pick the flowers that bloom on dense moss, clear fallen leaves, open chests and say hello to the animals that come by.

  4. Unlock new worlds

    Level up to open the castle ruin, the brook, a forgotten town, a garden on the Moon and an abyss of embers, each with its own flowers, animals and upgrades.

What's inside

Your real sky

Day and night, sun, rain and clouds follow the real time and weather where you are. Seasonal events bring pumpkins in autumn and gifts at Christmas.

6 worlds

From a forest clearing to the Moon. In the castle ruin you restore the castle stone by stone, until it is whole again.

36 flowers and 18 finds

Six rarities, from common to mythic. Every new piece raises your income in all your scenes.

18 animals

Robins, owls, otters, foxes, a moon rabbit and a magma blob. Each one appears in its own world, at its own time.

Your gardener

Change hair, hats, clothes and pets. Your character walks around, waters the moss and reacts to what happens.

Decorate your scene

Place stones, benches, pots, bridges, birdhouses and lanterns, lay stone paths and keep spare pieces in your inventory.

Friends

Share your friend code, visit other gardens, water your friends’ moss and send them a chest every day.

Something every day

A daily chest that grows with your streak, three daily missions, 147 achievements and a bird that sometimes drops a chest.

Plays anywhere

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, even offline. Your progress follows you to another device with a code.

Questions

Is tapmoss free?

Yes. You play in the browser at tapmoss.com, with nothing to download or install. Gems, the rarer currency, are earned by playing.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on phones, tablets and computers. From the browser menu you can add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app, even without internet.

Is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your device. Online it is also kept on the server, and you can continue on another device with a code from the settings.

Does the moss grow while I am away?

Yes. Your scenes keep earning coins for several hours while the game is closed, and longer with upgrades like the field notebook.

Why is it night in the game?

Because it is night where you are. tapmoss follows the real time and weather of your city, so rain, clouds and long nights all change how your garden looks.

How do I unlock new worlds?

By leveling up and buying them in Scenes: the castle ruin opens at level 20, the brook at 30, the forgotten town at 50, the Moon garden at 75 and the ember abyss at 100. Worlds you have not reached show up as a mystery.

Is there a rebirth or prestige?

Yes. From level 30 you can rebirth: coins and scene upgrades start over and the moss turns thin, but you get ancient spores that make everything earn more and grow faster, forever. Your level, gems, scenes and collection stay.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Share your friend code, visit their gardens, water their moss once a day and send them a chest. There is also a planet with the moss of every country and a top 20 for each one.

Who made tapmoss?

Dinis Sousa (dinissousa), in Portugal. New worlds, animals and events keep being added.
