Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who is responsible

tapmoss is made by Dinis Sousa, in Portugal. For any question about your data, write to diniscash@gmail.com.

What we keep and why

  • To save your game: the name you pick, your city (only the rough area), your country and your progress. We don't keep your exact location.
  • Email, only if you give it: so you can get your garden back on another device. News by email only if you tick the box, and at most once a month.
  • Notifications, only if you turn them on: the address your browser gives us to send you game notifications. Turn them off whenever you like.
  • Usage statistics: visits, play time, taps and country, so we know what to improve. We don't use them to identify you.

The game also keeps your progress in your browser, so it works offline.

Who sees what

Your profile and your scene are shown to your friends and, if you are in the ranking, to whoever views it. The planet only shows totals per country. We don't sell data to anyone.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details: we only get confirmation that the payment went through.

Ads

tapmoss may show ads from Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. In the European Union we ask for your consent first.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings. To learn how Google uses this data, see this Google page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. You can delete your online account in the game itself, in Settings, or by email. If you think we mishandled your data, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete it, your garden's data leaves the server.

Children

If you are under 13, ask an adult before giving your email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
