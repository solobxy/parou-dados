    Aftercare

    What you do in the first weeks makes a big difference to how it heals.

    Do

    • Wash your hands before touching the piercing.
    • Clean it twice a day with saline and dry it with clean paper.
    • Sleep on the other side, especially with cartilage piercings.
    • Keep the first jewellery in until it heals. Switching to a shorter bar is done with me.

    Avoid

    • Twisting or playing with the jewellery.
    • Pools, the sea and baths in the first weeks.
    • Alcohol, hydrogen peroxide and ointments I haven’t recommended.
    • Make-up, perfume or creams on the piercing.

    If it gets very swollen, hot or shows yellow or green pus, message me and see a doctor.

    Questions I get a lot

    Does it hurt?

    A little, and it’s over fast: a sting that lasts seconds. I explain every step and we only go ahead when you say so.

    Can I get several in one day?

    Yes. If it’s a lot, we start with a curation and decide together whether it’s better to do it in stages.

    Where is the studio?

    It depends on the day: I work in studios around Porto. The exact address comes in the message where I confirm your booking.

    I’m under 18. Can I?

    Under 18, only with a parent present and ID for both of you.

    Do you speak Portuguese?

    I do, but we can do everything in English: booking, the session and the aftercare.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Let me know on WhatsApp as early as you can, so I can give the slot to someone else. You can also cancel your request here on the site.
