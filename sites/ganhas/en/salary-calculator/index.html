Questions

How is net salary calculated in Portugal?

Gross salary minus 11 % Social Security and the IRS withholding tax from the official 2026 tables, which depend on your marital status and number of dependants. Holiday and Christmas pay are taxed separately.

Is this the final income tax?

No. Withholding is an advance on your income tax (IRS). The final tax is settled in the annual tax return, the following spring.

What is the net minimum wage in 2026?

The minimum wage is €920 gross a month in 2026. For a single person without dependants it is €818.80 net.

Does it include IRS Jovem or the non-habitual resident regime?

No. The calculator uses the standard withholding tables for residents of mainland Portugal. IRS Jovem and special regimes lower the tax and are not included.
