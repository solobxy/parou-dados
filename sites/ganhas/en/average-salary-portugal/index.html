Average salary in Portugal: €1,576 a month

In 2024, full-time private-sector employees in Portugal earned on average €1,576 gross a month, paid 14 times a year. Half earned less than €1,191.

Questions

What is the average salary in Portugal?

In 2024, the average gross monthly earnings of full-time private-sector employees were €1,576 (INE and GEP/MTSSS, Quadros de Pessoal). That is about €1,217 net for a single person without dependants, paid 14 times a year.

What is the median salary in Portugal?

€1,191 a month: half of full-time employees in mainland Portugal earned less than this (October 2024).

Is the salary paid 14 times a year?

Yes. Most employees in Portugal receive 14 payments: 12 monthly salaries plus holiday pay (summer) and Christmas pay.

Where are salaries highest?

In Greater Lisbon: €2,078 for men and €1,772 for women on average.

Source: INE, GEP/MTSSS, Quadros de Pessoal, outubro de 2024, publicado a 27/03/2026.
