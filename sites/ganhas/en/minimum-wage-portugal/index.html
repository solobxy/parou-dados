Minimum wage in Portugal 2026: €920

In 2026, the national minimum wage in Portugal is €920 gross a month, paid 14 times a year. Net, for a single person without dependants: €818.80. In 2027 it rises to €970.

Questions

What is the minimum wage in Portugal in 2026?

€920 gross a month, paid 14 times a year (€12,880 a year).

What is the net minimum wage in Portugal?

€818.80 a month for a single person without dependants: the minimum wage pays no IRS withholding and 11 % goes to Social Security.

What is the hourly minimum wage?

€5.31 an hour for a 40-hour week: (monthly wage × 12) ÷ (52 × 40), as set by article 271 of the Labour Code.

What will the minimum wage be in 2027?

€970 a month, €50 more than in 2026, under the social partners agreement.
